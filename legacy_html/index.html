About Us

A Legacy of Excellence in Higher Education

Established in 1951, the University of Karachi is Pakistan's premier public research university, spread across a 1,279-acre campus with nine faculties, 53 academic departments and 20 research institutes serving over 41,000 students.

Nine Faculties

From Science and Engineering to Arts, Law, Pharmacy and Islamic Studies.
