Overview & History

Introduction

Chairperson: Dr. Safia Urooj (Assoc. Prof.)

 It is the matter of great satisfaction that with the consistent efforts, the University of Karachi has introduced B.Ed. (Hons) 4 year program. The alternative paradigm of teacher education program consider structural changes in the system of teacher education, while redefining the teacher’s roles within the framework of the school education system.

Teacher Education courses in different countries extend for three or four years providing opportunity to improve upon teaching skills and the perception of teaching. Teacher education in Pakistan is for only one year, which does not expose to experiences necessary to enhance teaching skills and develop a positive perception of profession

Our goal is to equip the prospective teacher with skills essential for a lifelong learning.  We believe that a broad-based, flexible professional education provides the most appropriate formal preparation to:

  • Meet contemporary challenges,
  • Fulfill career goals,
  • Lead a purposeful, healthy and rewarding life, and
  • Serve society as a responsible citizen.
Vision

The University of Karachi envisions the leading University for professional excellence in Teacher Education.

Mission

Our mission is to provide exemplary programs that prepare teachers to make effective decisions in diverse contexts, promoting professional excellence within socio-cultural framework, bringing forth benefits to prospective teacher, community and the country.

Quality Policy

We envision a learning community marked by academic excellence, creative process, professional competence, and passionate Citizens, offering healing, hope and a high level of satisfaction in our diverse world.

Degree Offerings

Programs

Program offered
4-Year B.Ed. Elementary Program
A New Teacher Education Program for 21st Century Teachers
Eligibility & Allocated Seats

At least 50% marks in Intermediate (HSC) or equivalent examination. The structure of B.Ed. program is as follows;            

Courses Credit Hour
Compulsory course        19
Professional Course 15
Foundation Course  26
Content Course    24
Practicum   15
Total    135

The prime rationale of designing this 4-year B.Ed. program is to prepare the workforce ready to meet the challenges of globalization in term of command on subject-matter, instructional strategies, lesson planning, classroom  management, professional development of peer, to develop the linkage of classroom learning with community practices, enrichment of academic environment and fostering a high sense of professional ethics.
This 4-year B.Ed. program comprises eight semesters where variety of courses will be offered. Each subject in the program has two main components that is theory about the subject-matter and practicum. The courses are offered in terms of curriculum development, pedagogical knowledge and skills, research methodologies and assessment and evaluation as core elements of this course

Vision & Mission

Objectives

  • To prepare the best teachers through a rigorous teacher education program that seeks integration across disciplines.
  • To exhibit a reflective teaching model that develops excellence in teaching.
  • To display the strength to serve as collaborative change agents with sensitivity to an interdependent world.
  • To enable the candidates to respect and value cultural diversity.
  • To promote social harmony and peace building in learning communities.
  • To demonstrate resourcefulness and responsibility in educational settings influencing positive change.
