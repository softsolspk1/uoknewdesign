Department of Agriculture & Agribusiness Management

Faculty of Science

The Department launched its BS Program in Agriculture in 2004 and acquired departmental status in 2006, being renamed the Department of Agriculture and Agribusiness Management in 2008. It offers specializations in Agribusiness Management, Plant Protection and Plant Pathology.

* EST. 2006 * Faculty of Science

Specializations

Agribusiness, Plant Protection & Plant Pathology

Agribusiness Management

Combines agricultural science with management and economic principles for effective agribusiness execution.

Plant Protection

In-depth knowledge of pests and diseases affecting crops, vegetables, fruits and ornamental plants.

Plant Pathology

Eco-friendly disease and pest management strategies to help boost national crop yield.

Agribusiness is an important and ever-expanding industry in Pakistan, combining agricultural sciences with management skills and an understanding of economic and business principles. The program develops personal and transferrable skills alongside critical appreciation of techniques for effective agribusiness management. Proper pest and disease management strategies help increase crop yield and boost the national economy — students specializing in Plant Protection or Plant Pathology gain an in-depth understanding of pests and diseases affecting crops, vegetables, fruits and ornamental plants, and the eco-friendly management strategies used to address them.

Teaching & Assessment

Program Structure

During the first two years, courses covering different fields of agriculture build a broad background in the subject. From the third year, students choose a specialization in Agribusiness Management, Plant Protection or Plant Pathology. Assessment follows semester rules — midterm, make-up and terminal examinations plus lab examinations or assignments. In the final semester, an internship in a research or agribusiness-related organization is mandatory.

Careers

Career Opportunities

Agribusiness Management graduates find roles as managers, consultants, supply chain coordinators and accountants at national and multinational farm-business organizations, as well as opportunities for self-employment in farming and food sectors. Plant Protection and Plant Pathology graduates find opportunities at research organizations such as PARC, PCSIR, NIAB, NIA and ARI, at pesticide-related national and multinational organizations, or through their own pest and disease management consultancies.

Overview & History

Introduction

Chairperson: Prof. Dr. Saboohi Raza

The department of Agriculture launched its BS-Program in Agriculture in 2004 and acquired the status of the department in 2006. It was renamed as the Department of Agriculture and Agribusiness Management in 2008. The Department offers specializations in “Agribusiness management”, “Plant protection” and “Plant pathology”.

Agribusiness is an important and ever expanding industry in Pakistan. It combines the study of agricultural sciences with the management skills providing an understanding of economic and business principles and their application to Agribusiness. The program aims to develop a range of personal and transferrable skills along with the critical appreciation of the techniques to assist effective execution of agribusiness management function.

It cannot be denied that the implementation of proper pest and disease management strategies can be helpful in increasing the crop yield and ultimately boost the economy of the country. The courses offered for specialization in plant pathology or plant protection enable the students to acquire an in-depth knowledge of pest and diseases affecting crops, vegetables, fruits and ornamental plants in the country and make them aware of the management strategies to overcome the problem in an eco-friendly manner.


Teaching and Assessment

During the first two years, courses related to different fields of agriculture are taught to develop good background knowledge of the subject. From the third year, students can choose either Agribusiness Management, Plant Protection or Plant Pathology as their field of specialization. Assessment is done as per semester rules that include midterm, Make-up and terminal examinations, and a lab examination or assignment. In the final semester, internship in either a research or agribusiness related organization is mandatory.


Career Opportunities

Specialization in Agribusiness management is helpful in getting jobs in farm business related to national and multinational organizations as managers, consultants, supply chain coordinators and accountants. It also provides an opportunity to enter self-employment immediately after graduating, largely within the farming or food sectors.

Students specializing in plant protection or plant pathology have job opportunities in various research organizations like Department of plant protection, PARC, PCSIR, NIAB, NIA, ARI etc. They can also get jobs in plant and pesticide related national and multinational organizations or can initiate their own business to provide pest and disease management services to farmers.

Scholarly Contributions

Research

 

Research Activities

The department is actively engaged in research related to Entomology, Mycology, Plant Pathology and Agribusiness. Several research students are also enrolled in M.Phil. & Ph.D. programs.


Publications

The faculty members and research students regularly publish research papers in journals of national and international repute.

 
Infrastructure & Laboratories

Facilities

 

The department is well-equipped with modern teaching aids. It also has facilities for research on topics related to the fields of entomology, mycology, plant pathology and Agribusiness. The department's seminar library has a good collection of books related to agriculture and agribusiness that students can have easy access to.
