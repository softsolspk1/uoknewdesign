Overview & History

Introduction

Chairperson: Dr. Sadia Mahmood (Assoc. Prof.)

Previously known as the Department of Journalism, the Department of Mass Communication was established in 1955 with a diploma course designed for working journalists. The primary objective was to produce qualified journalists and media professionals to meet the growing needs of the national press and the Mass Communication industry. In 1962, the diploma program was replaced by a regular two-year M.A. program in light of the recommendations of the Pakistan Press Commission, with the aim of expanding and modernizing media education in the country.

The Department of Mass Communication continues to offer high-quality education in Journalism, Advertising, Public Relations and Electronic Media. Faculty members bring extensive professional experience from Print, Radio, Television, Advertising and Public Relations, ensuring students receive both theoretical and practical exposure to the media industry.

Keeping pace with the changing media landscape, the department has introduced new program including the BS Four Years Digital Media Marketing degree, which equips students with cutting- edge skills in content creation for social media platforms, SEO, analytics, and digital marketing strategies. This addition reflects the department’s commitment to preparing graduates for the demands of the modern digital economy and emerging career opportunities in the global media and marketing industries.

Teaching and Assessment

Apart from class lectures, students are required to do tutorials and assignments. It is also mandatory for each student to complete 180 hours of internships at media institutes. A certificate on satisfactory completion of internship enables students to apply for a marks sheet and a degree.

Career Opportunities

The Department of Mass Communication prepares students for a wide range of career opportunities in the rapidly evolving media and communication industry. Graduates can pursue roles in journalism as reporters, editors, feature writers, broadcast journalists, and documentary producers, or work in electronic media as television producers, radio broadcasters, news anchors, video editors, and camerapersons.

Exciting prospects also exist in advertising and public relations as creative directors, copywriters, media planners, brand managers, PR officers, and corporate communication specialists. In the digital space, students are equipped for careers as digital strategists, social media managers, SEO and SEM specialists, content creators, community managers, analytics experts, and e-commerce marketers. Many also choose to work as independent consultants, freelancers, or entrepreneurs, launching their own media and marketing ventures.

Infrastructure & Laboratories

Facilities

 

  • Computer Lab - The Department is equipped with modern Computer Labs featuring 25 computers, providing students with access to digital tools for research, design, media production, and practical training.

  • Audio Visual Room - A dedicated Audio-Visual Room equipped with multimedia facilities, enabling interactive lectures, presentations, workshops, and screenings that enhance students’ learning experience.

  • Radio Station - The Department operates its own Campus Radio FM 90.6, providing students with hands-on broadcasting experience. The facility includes two recording studios and one control room, where students are trained in radio production, programming, news reading, and on-air presentation under faculty supervision.

  • Television Studio - The Department houses a soundproof Television Studio equipped for the production of news bulletins, talk shows, programs, and short films. Supported by a fully functional Production Control Room (PCR), the studio provides students with practical training in on-camera presentation, studio production, direction, and technical operations, preparing them for careers in the electronic media industry.

  • News Lab - The Department’s News Lab is equipped with a multimedia News Editing Desk, where students gain practical training in news writing, editing, and layout design. It serves as a training base for producing newsletters and newspapers, giving students real newsroom experience.

  • Sarwar Naseem Hall - A fully air-conditioned seminar hall with a seating capacity of 100. Equipped with a modern sound system and multimedia facilities, it provides an ideal space for seminars, workshops, guest lectures, and student activities.

  • Hassan Riaz Library - The Hassan Riaz Library offers over 4,000 books, serving as a key resource for research and learning in Digital Media Marketing, Mass Communication, Journalism, Advertising and Public Relations.

  • Girls Prayer Room - A dedicated Girls’ Prayer Room is available in the department, providing students with a comfortable and convenient space for prayers.
 
Scholarly Contributions

Research

 

Journal of Mass Communication:
The Department publishes the bi-annual Journal of Mass Communication, which is HEC recognized Y- Category bilingual and peer-reviewed research journal. It provides scholars with a platform to explore the latest research in media and communication studies across a wide range of disciplines. https://jmcd- uok.com/index.php/jmcd/

 

Magazine:
MediaVerse – Departmental Magazine
MediaVerse is the Department of Mass Communication’s first-ever digital and print magazine, produced by students under the guidance of faculty. The magazine showcases the creativity, critical thinking, and journalistic skills of students through diverse content on social issues, culture, and global affairs. It serves as a platform for journalists to express their perspectives while gaining hands-on experience in writing, editing, graphic designing, advertising, and Public Relations. https://mediaverse.pk/

 

Other Publications:
Students publish newsletters and newspapers under faculty supervision, gaining hands-on experience in writing, editing, and production. Books are also published annually, providing updated and relevant material for students.
