Overview & History

Introduction

Chairperson: Prof. Dr. Rummana Zaheer

Welcome to the Department of Economics at the University of Karachi!

Introduction:
The Department of Economics is a cornerstone of the Faculty of Arts & Social Sciences, boasting a history rich in academic excellence and significant contributions to the field of economics. Established in the academic year 1950-51, following the enactment of the University of Karachi Act, the department began its journey at the old campus before relocating to its present, expansive location in 1960. The department was initially led by Professor Newman Joseph Adams, an American national who set the foundation for its future successes during his brief tenure as the first Chairman.

Teaching Philosophy and Curriculum:
Our teaching philosophy is a harmonious blend of theory and practical application, designed to equip students with a comprehensive understanding of economic principles and their real-world implications. The curriculum is delivered through a variety of methods including traditional lectures, interactive case studies, dynamic oral presentations, detailed written reports, and insightful field visits. This multi-faceted approach ensures that students gain both theoretical knowledge and practical skills.

Industry Integration and Continuous Assessment:
To enhance the learning experience, we regularly invite eminent guest speakers from leading economic and financial institutions to provide practical insights and real-world perspectives. These interactions bridge the gap between academia and industry, preparing students for the challenges of the professional world. Additionally, the department places a strong emphasis on continuous assessment to ensure that students are effectively assimilating the knowledge being imparted. This includes a range of evaluations from quizzes to extensive terminal examinations, as well as presentations and comprehensive written reports.

Alumni Success and Professional Preparation:
Graduates from the department are well-prepared to enter a wide array of professional fields. Our alumni have made significant strides in various sectors including banking, civil services (CSS), research, academia, and business, both domestically and internationally. The skills and knowledge acquired during their time at the department enable them to excel in their chosen careers and contribute meaningfully to their respective fields.

Research Initiatives and Projects:
In addition to our academic programs, the department is actively involved in research initiatives and projects that address contemporary economic challenges. Faculty members and students collaborate on research that spans a broad range of topics providing valuable insights that inform policy and practice.

Fostering Academic Community:
The department also fosters a vibrant academic community through seminars, workshops, and conferences. These events provide platforms for students, faculty, and visiting scholars to engage in intellectual exchange, discuss cutting-edge research, and explore new ideas in economics. This dynamic environment not only enhances the educational experience but also encourages the development of critical thinking and analytical skills.

Commitment to Excellence:
We are committed to maintaining our legacy of excellence and continue to strive towards advancing the field of economics through high-quality education, innovative research, and meaningful community engagement. The department remains dedicated to nurturing the next generation of economists who will lead and inspire in an ever-evolving global economy.

 

Academic Staff & Researchers

Faculty

The Department of Economics is renowned for its faculty, whose diverse backgrounds and extensive qualifications enrich the academic landscape. Each faculty member not only possesses degrees from esteemed institutions but also brings substantial teaching acumen and research prowess to the department. Their involvement in ongoing research projects is evident through publications in prestigious national and international journals, as well as presentations at conferences. Moreover, they actively contribute to the advancement of academic knowledge through books and monographs.

Covering a wide spectrum of disciplines, our faculty's expertise spans quantitative development analysis, international economics, microeconomics, macroeconomics, financial market analysis, environmental economics, Islamic economics, and more. This breadth ensures that students, both at the undergraduate and postgraduate levels, benefit from a comprehensive learning experience that fosters rigorous academic inquiry and encourages the development of robust research capabilities.

Beyond their teaching and research commitments, our faculty members are integral to the department's intellectual vibrancy. They frequently engage in workshops, seminars, and guest lectures, where they share their insights and engage in stimulating intellectual discourse. These activities not only enhance the academic experience for our students but also contribute significantly to the broader academic community.

Degree Offerings

Programs

Programs
The department offers a variety of programs designed to cater to the diverse interests and career aspirations of students. These programs are structured to provide a strong foundation in economic and finance theory, along with real-world applications.

Undergraduate Programs

  • BS Economics (Morning & Evening): This program offers a comprehensive understanding of economic principles, quantitative methods, and their applications in real-world scenarios.
  • BS Economics & Finance (BSEF) (Morning & Evening): This interdisciplinary program combines economics and finance, equipping students with the skills needed to excel in the financial sector.

Postgraduate Programs

  • M.Phil./M.S. in Economics (Morning): This advanced program focuses on in-depth economic analysis and research, preparing students for academic and professional careers.
  • Ph.D. in Economics (Morning): A research-intensive program designed for those who wish to contribute to the field of economics through original research.

Postgraduate Diploma

  • Postgraduate Diploma in Economics & Finance (PGDEF) (Evening only): This diploma program provides specialized knowledge in economics and finance, ideal for professionals seeking to enhance their expertise.

M.Phil. (Economics)
(With Course and Research Thesis)
Coursework Requirements

Coursework Composition:

    • MPhil candidates are required to complete 24 credit hours of coursework, and 6 credit hours dedicated to research work/thesis. Alternatively, candidates may opt to complete 30 credit hours of coursework; however, upon completion of 30 credit hours, they will be awarded an MS (Master of Science) degree instead of an MPhil degree.

Coursework Composition:

    • The coursework must be relevant to the MPhil degree and should be offered through regular on-campus classes taught by full-time faculty members.
    • The coursework should cover the foundational and advanced topics necessary for the field of study.

Research Component:

    • If the program includes a research component (thesis), the university must establish a policy for thesis defense and evaluation through its statutory bodies.

Degree Completion Timeline:

    • The MPhil degree should be completed within a minimum of 1.5 years (three regular semesters) and a maximum of 4 years (eight regular semesters). Extensions may be granted in cases of force majeure.

Additional Points:

    • Universities are allowed to transfer credits from other institutions following the HEC's guidelines, provided the coursework aligns with the program's requirements.

Eligibility Criteria:

    • Students who have completed their BS in the following fields are eligible to apply for MPhil admission: Economics, Economics and Finance, Commerce, Business Administration, Public Administration, Mathematics, Statistics, and Physics

Passing Criteria:

    • A minimum CGPA of 2.6 is required for successful completion.

Ph.D. (Economics)
Coursework and Residency Requirement

1. Credit Hours:

  • 18 Credit Hours of coursework are required if the student is from the same discipline.
  • These courses must be delivered through regular on-campus classes taught by full-time faculty members.
  • The courses should preferably be of the 800 level or higher.
  • The PhD degree should primarily focus on research rather than merely accumulating credit hours.

2. Comprehensive Examination

  • Completion Timeline:
    • The coursework, comprehensive exam, and defense of the synopsis/research proposal must be completed within the first six semesters (3 years) of enrollment in the PhD program. The maximum duration allowed for completing the PhD, as per HEC policy, is 8 years.
    • Failure to comply may result in the cancellation of the student's registration, and transcripts for completed coursework may be issued.
    • In the event of noncompliance due to force majeure (unforeseen circumstances), the university may consider exceptions according to prescribed procedures.
  • Exam Guidelines:
    • The comprehensive exam should assess the student's conceptual knowledge of the discipline.
    • The exam will cover courses studied, preferably at the graduate level, and will be conducted as one composite question paper.
    • The evaluation will be on an aggregate basis, expressed as pass/fail, and will not be graded.

3. Doctoral Dissertation

  • Research Area:
    • The dissertation must reflect relevance, credibility, effectiveness, and legitimacy of the research.
    • The research area should:
      • Address community needs at regional and local levels and align with the national research agenda.
      • Focus on basic and pure research.
      • Include emerging areas of research, preferably aligned with sustainable development goals (SDGs).
  • Dissertation Requirements
    • The doctoral dissertation must be an original and innovative contribution to knowledge, aimed at solving socio-economic problems.

Infrastructure & Laboratories

Facilities & Awards

Department Environment
The Department of Economics provides a conducive environment for learning and research. It features large lecture halls and classrooms, a computer lab, a research lab, and a seminar library. There is ample office space for faculty members and secretarial staff.

Seminar Library

The Department of Economics is an ideal place for study and research, offering a range of facilities to support academic and research activities. The department has its own seminar library, which boasts a comprehensive collection of around 2000 books and periodicals related to economics and related fields. For materials not found within the library, students and faculty can utilize inter-library loan facilities and access other major libraries, including the main library and the research library of AERC at the University of Karachi. The department's library is well-equipped to provide course materials as well as reference literature essential for students throughout their studies.

Computer Lab

The department prioritizes student facilities, such as a computer lab with 30 computers dedicated to economic analysis, financial modeling, and statistical applications. This lab plays a crucial role in improving students' technical skills through practical learning experiences that reinforce theoretical knowledge. The lab fosters collaborative projects, simulations, and data analysis, which enhance critical thinking and problem-solving capabilities crucial for navigating the modern economic environment successfully.

Research Lab

The department has expanded its facilities with a dedicated research lab for M.Phil. and Ph.D. students, equipped with 20 advanced computers. This new addition enhances the department's research capabilities, providing a supportive environment for in-depth data analysis and interdisciplinary collaboration. The lab underscores the department's commitment to fostering academic excellence and innovative research in economics and related fields, empowering graduate students to pursue rigorous scholarly inquiry and contribute impactful insights to their disciplines.

Extracurricular Activities

The department emphasizes the importance of extracurricular activities for the overall development of students. Various sports such as cricket, football, and hockey are available, with inter-department tournaments held annually. The department also organizes study tours and visits to financial institutions, which supplement theoretical knowledge with practical exposure.
The department fosters a close-knit community where students have great accessibility to the administration and faculty. The interactions between faculty and students help in personal and professional development, making students more confident, mature, and dynamic.

Awards

Through the generosity of philanthropists, the department awards Gold Medals to students who secure top positions at different levels, recognizing and encouraging academic excellence.

Achievements

The department is renowned for its high academic standards, leading to the establishment of the AERC. The AERC, supported by a grant from the Sindh Government and later by the Ford Foundation, has become a leading institute for social and economic research. This achievement underscores the department's commitment to academic excellence and research.
