Center for Digital Forensic Science & Technology

For the first time in Pakistan, the University of Karachi offers digital forensic education at the undergraduate, graduate, postgraduate, MS and Ph.D. levels. CDFST supports law enforcement agencies — police, Rangers, NAB, FIA and CPLC — by connecting the crime scene and judicial system through impartial, expert scientific evidence, contributing to a faster and more effective criminal justice system.

  • Digital Forensic Education
  • Law Enforcement Support
  • Advanced Digital Evidence Systems
  • Career Pathways in Forensic Science

About the Centre

  • Director: Dr. Nadeem Mahmood
  • Part of the University of Karachi's digital forensics initiative

Focus Areas

  • Programs span BSc (Hons), Masters, M.Phil and Ph.D. levels, applying digital forensic science across criminal law, civil law, product liability, medical integrity, highway safety, environmental protection and national security.

Role in the Justice System

  • 01Supports law enforcement agencies in meeting the digital evidence expectations of government and community.
  • 02Applies legislation including the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act and the Investigation for Fair Trial Act, 2012.
  • 03Trains a new generation of digital forensic analysts and experts for Pakistan's justice system.

Contact & Leadership

  • Director: Dr. Nadeem Mahmood
  • Institute: Center for Digital Forensic Science & Technology, University of Karachi
  • General enquiries: Contact the University

Economic & Social Impact

CDFST aims to strengthen Pakistan's economy by creating new job opportunities and a clear specialization path for students, while supplying qualified digital forensic manpower to public and private sector organizations.
