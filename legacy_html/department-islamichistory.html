Department of Islamic History

Faculty of Arts & Social Sciences

The Department of Islamic History may be regarded as one of the oldest departments in the University of Karachi. It was established in 1953 to provide facilities for advanced studies and original research in the field of Islamic History & Culture, with Professor Dr. Amir Hassan Siddiqui as its first and founder Head.

* EST. 1953 * Islamic History & Culture

The department's specific research areas cover all aspects and periods of Islamic History, supported by dedicated faculty and departmental activities that promote research and scholarship in the field.

"Providing advanced study and original research across all aspects and periods of Islamic History."

Overview & History

Introduction

Chairman: Prof. Dr. Muhammad Zubair

Department of Islamic History may be regarded as one of the oldest departments in Karachi University. It was established in 1953 with a view to provide facilities for advance studies and original research in the field of Islamic History & Culture. Professor Dr. Amir Hassan Sidddiqui was the first and founder-Head of the Department.

Career Opportunities

There is a great scope for the students of Islamic History in the fields of Social Sciences, Political Science, International Relations and Sociology. They have a very bright future in the profession of teaching, research and media.

Specific Research Areas:
All aspects and periods of Islamic History.

Infrastructure & Laboratories

Facilities

The Seminar Library possesses a collection of about 3000 books. It provides reading and research facilities to the students of Islamic History.

Events & Engagement

Activities

Co-curricular Activities:
Co-curricular activities are necessary to encourage and polish the talents of the younger generation, and to infuse in them the spirit of social and cultural advancement. The department has an Advisory Committee to guide and organize activities like picnics, excursion tours, historical visits, quiz and essay competitions etc.
