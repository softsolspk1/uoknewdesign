GOVERNANCE

Administration of the University

The University of Karachi is led by the Vice Chancellor and administered through the Registrar's Office, the Syndicate and a network of directorates and offices that manage academics, admissions, finance, affiliations and student affairs. Together these bodies run the day-to-day governance of one of Pakistan's largest universities.
